# Schnaps 0.6.3 Beta 1

- Choose Stable or Beta in Settings → About Schnaps → Updates.
- Beta builds receive early updates through a signed Sparkle channel.
- New transparent liquid-glass bottle icon with blueprint measurement lines.

Switching back to Stable waits for a newer stable build; it does not downgrade the installed app.

Universal for Apple Silicon and Intel. Requires macOS 14 or later.
