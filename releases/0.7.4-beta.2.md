## Compact tool overlay

The tool overlay is now a compact, draggable icon strip with no title bar, traffic lights, or resize controls. Open the inspector for native controls in a popover. When enabled, the toolbar returns after relaunch without opening Settings alongside it.

## Editable global shortcuts

Change or clear all 12 global shortcuts in Settings → Shortcuts. Bindings are saved, native menu labels update immediately, and duplicate or unavailable combinations are rejected. Restore Default Shortcuts resets them all.

This beta includes the Mac design and inspection tools introduced in beta 1. The Tool Overlay remains hidden by default; enable it from the menu bar.

Universal for Apple Silicon and Intel. Requires macOS 14 or later.
