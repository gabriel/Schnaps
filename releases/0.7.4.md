## Mac design and inspection tools

Add rulers, guides, frames, grids/mockup overlays, and screen templates. Inspect pixels and colors with Loupe and Crosshair, work with Unicode and text conversions, and preview images, windows, and responsive webpages.

## Compact optional toolbar

Enable Tool Overlay in the menu bar for a draggable icon strip without window chrome. Native controls open in a popover. The toolbar starts hidden by default and remembers whether you enabled it across restarts.

## Editable shortcuts and easier dismissal

Change or clear all 12 global shortcuts in Settings → Shortcuts, with conflict detection and restore defaults. Press Escape while using the design tools to dismiss the most recently shown item; repeat to clear the screen. Dismissed markers retain their saved geometry.

## Native window fixes

Text Inspector and content previews accept clicks and typing when opened over design marks. The toolbar’s Text button positions its window directly below the toolbar with an 8-point gap, adjusting to keep both on screen.

Promoted from the tested 0.7.4-beta.3 feature set. Universal for Apple Silicon and Intel. Requires macOS 14 or later.
