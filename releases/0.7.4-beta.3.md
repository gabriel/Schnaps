## Escape to clear tools

Press Escape to dismiss the most recently shown design marker, Loupe, or Crosshair. Repeat to clear the screen one item at a time. Holding Escape does not clear additional tools. Dismissed markers keep their saved geometry, and the toolbar stays enabled independently.

## Text Inspector fixes

Text Inspector now accepts clicks and typing when opened while editing design marks. Opening Text, Image & Window Preview, or Responsive Preview makes the marks click-through before showing the native window.

Clicking Text on the toolbar opens its window centered directly below the toolbar with an 8-point gap. Placement adjusts to keep both windows on screen.

This beta also includes the compact toolbar and editable global shortcuts from beta 2.

Universal for Apple Silicon and Intel. Requires macOS 14 or later.
