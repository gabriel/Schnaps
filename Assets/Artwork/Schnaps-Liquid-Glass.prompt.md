## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a highly refined macOS app icon / transparent brand mark for Schnaps, a precision screen measurement app. Create ONE new icon: an empty schnapps bottle combining liquid glass material with blueprint drafting. Actual transparent alpha background, no backdrop tile, no rounded square, no ground plane. Subject: strong simple bottle silhouette with a short narrow neck, precisely chamfered shoulders, gently rounded rectangular body and thick optically clear glass base, restrained clear glass stopper. Front elevation with a slight dimensional bevel, almost orthographic. Sculptural optical glass, beautifully controlled refraction, clear hollow interior, pale ice-blue edge tint, crisp white specular edges, a few deep blue refractive accents. The material should feel like Apple's liquid glass: substantial transparent lens-like glass, very premium and convincing, not chrome or plastic. Integrate sparse precise cyan-blue blueprint linework: one vertical dimension rail beside bottle, one horizontal dimension rail beneath, tiny arrowheads and extension ticks aligned to bottle endpoints; a delicate partial construction contour traces one shoulder and base. It is a bottle being precisely measured, not a bottle inside a wireframe cage. Quiet technical elegance, sophisticated industrial design, bold readable silhouette at small icon sizes. Center the bottle and measuring rails as one balanced square composition with ample transparent breathing room. No liquid in bottle, no shot glass, no lettering, no numbers, no labels, no grid wallpaper, no neon glow, no cartoon illustration, no decorative sparkles, no mockup, no checkerboard pattern baked into image. Transparent background is essential; preserve subtly translucent glass surfaces and opaque enough rim highlights for visibility on light and dark backgrounds.
